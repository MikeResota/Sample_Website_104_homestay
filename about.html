Our Story

Family roots, warm hospitality & the aroma of fresh bread

Since 2018

A Home Away From Home

Serenity Homestay began as a family dream — to share our love of quiet countryside living and the joy of baking with travelers from around the world.

What started as a single guest room and a small kitchen oven has grown into a beloved destination known for its thoughtful hospitality, garden views, and bakery that fills the air with the scent of sourdough and cinnamon every morning.

We still bake every loaf by hand, welcome every guest personally, and believe that the best stays are the ones that feel like coming home.

The Bakery

Baked Fresh Every Morning

Our bakery is the heart of Serenity. Long before guests wake up, the ovens are already warm. We mill some of our own flour, culture our own starters, and use seasonal fruit from nearby orchards.

Whether you're staying with us or just stopping by for a loaf and coffee, you'll taste the difference that care makes.
