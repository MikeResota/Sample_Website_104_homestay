A Home Away From Home

Serenity Homestay began as a simple dream — to create a place where guests feel the same warmth and comfort they would in their own home. Nestled in Green Valley, our family has welcomed travelers seeking quiet, connection, and genuine care.

Every room is thoughtfully prepared, every meal shared with intention, and every detail considered so you can truly rest.
